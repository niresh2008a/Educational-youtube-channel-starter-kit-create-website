Thumbnail formula

One big idea + 2–5 strong words + readable contrast + a recognizable visual. Avoid clutter.

📈

Track learning

Review clicks, watch time, retention, comments, and the questions viewers repeatedly ask.

30-day content roadmap

A simple launch rhythm that keeps the channel focused.

Week 1 — Foundation

Define your audience, channel promise, 3 content pillars, and 10 video ideas.

Week 2 — Publish

Publish 2 useful tutorials and 3 short-form tips. Improve your opening hook each time.

Week 3 — Learn

Study retention and comments. Double down on topics viewers understand and request.

Week 4 — Improve

Refresh thumbnails/titles where needed and plan the next 10 videos from audience questions.
